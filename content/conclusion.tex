\section{Answers to the Research Questions}
\label{sec:conclusion:1}

\paragraph{RQ1.} \emph{\rqOneText} Behaviour can be represented at the unit that a deployment
exposes, but the representation must also admit legitimate change. On a benign industrial day,
all nine false alarm episodes had benign causes, five of them engineering work or planned device
restarts. Chapter~\ref{chap:anomaly} closes gap~\gapref{1} at the implementation level.

\paragraph{RQ1.1.} \emph{\rqOneOneText} Each node learns the relations observed while a service
starts and reports any relation absent from its graph, which the site owner may then legitimate.
Detection added about 6~ms to a 24~ms round trip, and this cost did not grow between ten and forty
services.

\paragraph{RQ1.2.} \emph{\rqOneTwoText} \nadics{} fixes the interfaces around the learning stage,
so that estimators can be exchanged by name. Under a session-disjoint split of the S7-300 corpus
from \iuno{}, a random forest reached an attack $F_1$ of 94.50 per cent at a record false-positive
rate of 0.484 per cent. On 393,679 later SWaT timestamps withheld from model development, a
GAN-based detector added at the same interface flags 92.00 per cent of anomalous timestamps at a
false-positive rate of 5.12 per cent.

\paragraph{RQ2.} \emph{\rqTwoText} On CICAndMal2017, fusion added one or two points of detection
$F_1$ over the better single modality, and between minus two and plus three points in category
classification. Network flow led program code in detection and trailed it in category
classification. The combination therefore helps detection slightly and category classification
only with some learners, which closes gap~\gapref{2} for this corpus and protocol.

\paragraph{RQ3.} \emph{\rqThreeText} With identical inputs, every hypergraph operator exceeded both
pairwise operators in all four task and corpus combinations, and HGNN+ led GraphSAGE by 3.8 to 6.7
points of accuracy. \hgann{} led the non-attentive operators by 1.2 to 4.1 points in three
combinations and was level in the fourth. Its attention can separate hypergraphs that share a
clique expansion, although within one layer it ranks the hyperedges of every method in one shared
order. These comparisons of complete systems close gap~\gapref{3}.

\paragraph{RQ4.} \emph{\rqFourText} The SAFAIR work translated the adversary model into an
evaluation protocol, realised as a contest and then as a modular benchmark that combines attacks
and defences by configuration. Reproducible measurement also requires a fixed prediction objective
and a valid perturbation set. The accuracy-loss score used for ranking is indifferent to utility:
any constant classifier has zero loss under label-preserving attacks, and under PGD the defence
with zero loss was 12.9 points less accurate than adversarial training. Gap~\gapref{4} is closed
for building and scoring such a benchmark; the four contest rows do not establish a general
relation between clean performance and robustness.

\section{Summary of Contributions}
\label{sec:conclusion:2}

For IoT services, the candidate contributed to the concept and the measurement design of a
communication-graph model that each node keeps current as services change, at a measured cost in
memory and latency. For industrial control systems, he designed and implemented \nadics{} with its
S7 and PROFINET extractor, whose 63-field schema reaches from flow statistics to S7 function codes,
and he produced the labelled S7-300 corpus of 25 attack episodes. The GAN-based detector for SWaT
process data is also his work.

\hybroid{} contributes a flow pipeline that reduces the traffic of an application to
\netfeatures{} features, together with a comparison of each modality with their fusion on the same
samples. \hgann{} lifts the function-call graph to a hypergraph under three construction rules and
learns membership weights by attention. The third rule builds a hyperedge from the backward slice
of each security-sensitive call site and weights it by the transmission and access calls in that
slice. Chapter~\ref{chap:hgann} restores the construction algorithm and analyses the attention
operator, and it shows that the hypergraph grows linearly with the numbers of methods and slices.
The SAFAIR contest toolkit and benchmark, both published in the candidate's name, form the
evaluation contribution. Chapter~\ref{chap:advml} derives how the accuracy-loss score behaves under
constant predictions and how label reversal after an alarm changes accuracy. In
Chapter~\ref{chap:discussion}, the observations each representation requires are related to the
conclusions its evaluation supports.

\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion:3}

The first open item is the \hgann{} ablation, which isolates the effect of attention: the
non-attentive operator over the identical fused hyperedges, and \hgann{} over topological
hyperedges alone, on both corpora and under a stated normalisation convention. Both Android
systems then need time-ordered splits and attacks through valid package transformations, with
clean and attacked performance reported under one protocol. For \hybroid{}, such an attack would
test whether unreachable injected calls leave its traffic features unchanged.
